01The problem

Small drones are cheap, quiet, and already inside the fence. A camera feed is not a defence. A waypoint loop is not a defence. A detector that publishes boxes and then shrugs is not a defence.

The hard part is not “run YOLO.” It is closing the loop from pixel to chase to a deliberate, auditable decision — without coupling the brain to PX4, Gazebo, or a particular LLM.

What breaks

  • Perception that never commands the vehicle
  • Autopilots flying a box blind
  • Humans who must notice, decide, and click in time
  • One MAVSDK link: when it stalls, safety stalls

What we insist on

  • One launch file: sim, flight, vision, UI
  • Brain talks actions and services, never MAVLink
  • Engagement is gated. Default is a human
  • Battery and airborne can veto a chase

The operational sentence

Stay on station. Notice a non-cooperative aircraft. Get close enough to act. Only then spend the shot. Miss detection and you patrol forever. Detect without lock and you write a log. Lock without an approval gate and you have built a weapon, not a system. Approve without a report and you cannot tell anyone what happened.

Wingbreaker is that full sentence in simulation: typed interfaces, a supervised flight gateway, a state machine you can read in one sitting, and an operator surface that makes the decision visible.

03The solution

One supervised state machine. A camera that estimates GPS from a box. A flight node that actually arrives. A fire service that cannot be called until someone — or a policy — approves.

Idleboot ConnPX4 up Patrolbox @ 65 m Lockfly + orbit Decide30 s window Engage/fire Reportaudit row Patrolresume

Denied, timeout, or a failed lock all land in REPORT, then cool down 10 s. Detections are ignored off-PATROL, on the ground, or when safe_to_fly is false.

See — detector

Live YOLO on yolo26n_DroneDetection.pt, every 3rd frame, strongest drone box ≥ 0.35. Brain chases only above 0.55. Pixel→GPS is a bearing through 114.6° HFOV at a fixed 60 m range — ownship altitude copied. Deliberately naive. Not a tracker.

Go — flight gateway

DroneGateway: one asyncio loop, one MAVSDK System, dedicated gRPC port, reconnects forever. Airborne = landed-state OR alt > 3 m (PX4 FW keeps LandedState UNDEFINED in cruise). Arrival radius 100 m — L1 loiters, it will not park on a pin. Goals cancel the instant a valid detection arrives.

Ask — three modes

ModeWho decides
humanDashboard Approve or CLI. Default. The honest demo.

Optional · four things worth knowing

04 · Operator loop

stdlib HTTP node. SSE state, MJPEG with YOLO box, Approve as approver="human". QGC is a sidecar (noVNC), not a ROS node. Silence for 30 s is a deny.

05 · Safety as a second voice

Second MAVSDK link. Battery < 20% vetoes chase. Unknown (−1) treated as safe so a cold sim is not grounded. Dedicated ports so a stale mavsdk_server cannot look alive.

06 · The world

Reaper-style ownship, gimbal along the nose. Intruder is a 3× static X500 teleported with gz service — no physics. App nodes wait for a real camera frame, not a publisher.

07 · Honest limits

E2E verified: PATROL→LOCK→Approve→ENGAGE→REPORT. Fire is a stub. Range is assumed. LLM is a mock plus a hook. Next: real range, a live provider, track IDs, HITL.
